# Understand WhatsApp Status

The WhatsApp Status feature lets you share different types of updates with a selected audience. Status updates disappear 24 hours after you post them. 

**Content types you can share on Status**

- **Text:** Share a text message.
- **Photos and videos:** Share photos or video clips.
- **Voice recordings:** Record and share a voice message.
- **Links:** Share website links.
- **Music:** Share a song you recommend.
- **Layout:** Share multiple photos in a layout style.
- **AI-generated images:** Generate and share images using Meta AI.

You can also personalize your Status using captions, emojis, stickers, GIFs, and drawings. These options differ depending on the type of Status you want to share. 

**How Status works**

You can share a new Status by tapping **Add status** in the **Updates** tab. Each status updates remain available for 24 hours. During this period, you can share multiple Status updates.

Your contacts can view, like, reply, or react to your Status updates from the **Updates** tab. You can see who has viewed or liked your updates. 

**Managing Status**

* **Edit:** After you post a Status, you cannot change the shared media or selected audience. You can edit the caption within 15 minutes of posting the Status.
* **Delete:** You can delete a Status before it expires. However, you cannot delete multiple Status updates at once.
* **Save:** You can save your Status updates to your device and view them in your gallery or Google Photos. However, you cannot save your contacts' Status updates.
  
**Privacy and audience**

WhatsApp protects your Status using end-to-end encryption. This means that not even WhatsApp can access the content of your Status.

By default, all your contacts can see your Status updates. You can control who can see your updates using the **Status privacy** setting. WhatsApp also lets you select your audience before ypu post a Status. Further, you can mention up to five contacts or groups in your Status. For more information, see [Individual and group mentions](/Reference/Individual_and_group_mentions.md). 

WhatsApp privately notifies the contacts or groups you mention. Other contacts cannot see whom you mentioned. The mentioned contacts or group members can reshare your Status. Their audience can only see the Status content. WhatsApp does not share your name, profile information, or phone number with that audience.
