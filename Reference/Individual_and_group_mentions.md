# Individual and group mentions in a Status

When you mention individual contacts or groups in your Status, they can view it even if you have not included them in your Status audience. The table below compares individual mentions and group mentions. 

**Differences between individual and group mentions**

Type of mention | Who can you mention? | How are recipients notified? | Who can see the mention? | Who can edit and reshare?
---- | ---- | ---- | ---- | ---- |
Individual | A WhatsApp user | WhatsApp sends a notification to the mentioned contact. <br><br>A message also appears in the contact's chat with you. Tapping this message opens your Status.| Only the mentioned contact. | Only the mentioned contact. |
Group | A WhatsApp group  | A message appears in the existing group chat. <br><br> Tapping the message opens your Status. | All members of the mentioned group. | All members of the mentioned group. |


**Note:** The contacts you mention in your Status can edit and reshare its content with their own audience. They cannot edit the original content of your Status. When they reshare your Status, WhatsApp does not share your name, phone number, or profile information with their audience.
