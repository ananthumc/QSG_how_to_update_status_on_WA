# Post an AI-generated image as a Status

You can create and share images generated by Meta AI as your Status. Follow the steps below to share an AI-generated image as your WhatsApp Status.

To post an AI-generated image as your Status,

1. On WhatsApp, tap **Updates**.
2. On the **Updates** screen, perform one of the following actions:
   *   Tap **Add status**.
   *   Tap the camera icon at the bottom-right corner.
   A screen displays thumbnails of the media on your device, along with the available Status options.
3. Tap **AI images** from the list of Status options.
4. On the next screen, tap **Create image** or tap the **Describe an image...** text field at the bottom of the screen.
**Note:** You can also select from the suggested images on screen.
5.   Enter the description of the image you want Meta AI to generate and tap the generate (sparkles) icon. 
  Wait while Meta AI generates the image.
6. After Meta AI generates the image, edit it using the following options:
    * **Edit:** Tap the **Describe edits...** text field and describe the edits you want Meta AI to make.
    * **Restyle:** Tap **Restyle** to apply different image styles, moods, lighting, and colors.
    * **Regenerate:** Tap the regenerate icon (circular arrow) at the bottom-left of the screen to generate a new image based on your same prompt.   

7. Tap the tick icon at the bottom-right of the screen to finalize the image.
8. (Optional) On the next screen, customize your Status using the following options:   
    * **Music:** Tap the music icon to add a song.
    * **Crop/rotate:** Tap the crop/rotate icon to crop or rotate the image.
    * **Sticker:** Tap the sticker icon to add stickers or emojis.
    * **Text:** Tap **Aa** to add text on the image. 
    You can change the font, size, color, layout, and background of this text.
    * **Draw:** Tap the pencil icon to draw on the image using different colors. You can adjust the color and line size.
    * **Add yours sticker:** Tap **Add sticker** to add a sticker that lets your audience create their own AI-generated image using your prompt.
    * **Caption:** Tap **Add a caption…** to add a line of description.
9. (Optional) Tap **@** to mention up to five contacts in your Status.
10. (Optional) On the same screen, select your Status audience by performing the following actions:   
                 
    a. In the bottom-left corner of the screen, tap **Status**.   
    
    &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The **Choose audience** bottom sheet appears.   

    b. Select one of the following options:      

     * **My contacts:** Allow all your WhatsApp contacts to view your Status.
     * **My contacts except:** Exclude specific contacts from viewing your Status.
     *  **Only share with…:** Select specific contacts who can view your Status.   
  
    **Note:** Tap the two-arrow icon next to **Share** to allow your audience to share your Status.

    c. Tap **Share**.
11. Tap the share icon at the bottom-right corner.

The AI-generated image appears as your Status. Your audience can view and react to it.
