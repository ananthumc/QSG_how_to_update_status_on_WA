# Post a layout Status

You can share more than one photo as your Status using the **Layout** option. The procedure below explains how you can post a layout Status.

**Prerequisite**
At least two photos available on device.

To post a layout Status,

1. On WhatsApp, tap **Updates**.
2. On the **Updates** screen, perform one of the following actions:
   *   Tap **Add status**.
   *   Tap the camera icon at the bottom-right corner.
   A screen displays thumbnails of the media on your device, along with the available Status options.
3. Tap **Layout** from the list of Status options.
   The **Start layout** screen appears, displaying the available media.
4. Select up to six photos by performing one of the following actions:
   * Select photos from the media displayed.
   * Tap the **Recents** drop-down menu and select photos from your desired album.
   * Tap the folder icon at the bottom-right corner and select photos from the displayed media.
  **Note:** Choose a minimum of two photos.
5. Tap the check-mark icon at the bottom-right corner.
**Note:** Tap a photo to replace it and select a new one.
6. On the next screen, tap the layout icons to try different layouts for your photos.
   **Note:** If there are not enough photos to make a layout, WhatsApp prompts you to add more.
7. After you select a layout style, tap **Done**.   
8. (Optional) On the same screen, customize your photo using the following options:   
    * **Music:** Tap the music icon to add a song.
    * **Sticker:** Tap the sticker icon to add stickers or emojis.
    * **Text format:** Tap **Aa** to add text on the photo. You can change the font, size, color, layout, and background of this text.
    * **Draw:** Tap the pencil icon to draw on your photo using different colors. You can adjust the color and line size.
    * **Caption:** Tap **Add a caption…** to add a line of description.
9. (Optional) Tap **@** to mention up to five contacts in your Status.
10.  (Optional) On the same screen, select your Status audience by performing the following actions:                 
    a. In the bottom-left corner of the screen, tap **Status**.    
    &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The **Choose audience** bottom sheet appears.   
    b. Select one of the following options:      

     * **My contacts:** Allow all your WhatsApp contacts to view your Status.
     * **My contacts except:** Exclude specific contacts from viewing your Status.
     *  **Only share with…:** Select specific contacts who can view your Status.   
      **Note:** Tap the two-arrow icon next to **Share** to allow your audience to share your Status.
      
       c. Tap **Share**.

12.  Tap the share icon at the bottom-right corner.

Your photo layout appears as your Status. Your audience can view and react to it.
