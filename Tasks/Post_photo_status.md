# Post a photo Status   

WhatsApp allows you to post different types of media as your Status. The procedure below explains how to post a photo as your Status.   

**Prerequisite**
A photo on your device if you want to post an existing photo.   

To post a photo as your Status,
1.	On WhatsApp, tap **Updates**.
2.	On **Updates**, perform one of the following actions:   
    * Tap **Add status**.
    *  Tap the camera icon at the bottom-right corner. 
A screen displays thumbnails of the media on your device, along with the available Status options.
**Note:** Tap **Layout**  to post up to 6 photos.
For more information, see [Post a layout Status](/Post_layout_status.md).
3.	Select a photo using one of the following methods:   
    *    **From media:** Select a photo from the displayed media.
    *    **From album:** Tap **Recents** and select a photo from your desired album.
    *    **From photo picker:** Tap the folder icon at the bottom-right corner and select a photo from the displayed media.
    *    **From** **camera:** Tap **Camera** and click a photo.
    *    **From camera screen:** Tap **Camera** and then tap the gallery icon at the bottom-left corner. Perform one of the following actions:
         *    Select a photo from the displayed media.
         *    Tap **Recents** and select a photo from your desired album.
         *    Tap the folder icon at the bottom and select a photo from the displayed media.
4.	(Optional) After selecting the photo, customize it using the following options:   
    * **Music:** Tap the music icon to add a song.
    * **Crop/rotate:** Tap the crop/rotate icon to crop or rotate your photo.
    * **Sticker:** Tap the sticker icon to add stickers or emojis.
    * **Text format:** Tap **Aa** to add text on the photo. 
    You can change the font, size, color, layout, and background of this text.
    * **Draw:** Tap the pencil icon to draw on your photo using different colors. You can adjust the color and line size.
    * **Caption:** Tap **Add a caption…** to add a line of description.
5. (Optional) Tap **@** to mention up to five contacts in your Status.
6. (Optional) On the same screen, select your Status audience by performing the following actions:   
                 
    a. In the bottom-left corner of the screen, tap **Status**.   
    
    &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The **Choose audience** bottom sheet appears.   

    b. Select one of the following options:      

     * **My contacts:** Allow all your WhatsApp contacts to view your Status.
     * **My contacts except:** Exclude specific contacts from viewing your Status.
     *  **Only share with…:** Select specific contacts who can view your Status.   
  
    **Note:** Tap the two-arrow icon next to **Share** to allow your audience to share your Status.

    c. Tap **Share**.

7. Tap the share icon at the bottom-right corner.   

Your photo appears as your Status. Your audience can view and react to it.
