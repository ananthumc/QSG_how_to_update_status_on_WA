# Post a song as a Status   

You can select a song from WhatsApp's music library and use it as your Status. The procedure below explains how to do this.

To post a song as a Status,

1. On WhatsApp, tap **Updates**.
2. On the **Updates** screen, perform one of the following actions:
   *   Tap **Add status**.
   *   Tap the camera icon at the bottom-right corner.
    A screen displays thumbnails of the media on your device, along with the available Status options.
3. Tap **Music**.
4. On the next screen, find a song by performing one of the following actions:
    * In the search bar at the top of the screen, enter the song name.
    The songs that match the entered name appear below the search bar.
    * Tap one of the following options:
      * **Suggested:** Browse WhatsApp-recommended songs.
      * **Mood:**   Browse songs by mood.
      * **Genre:** Browse songs by genre.
      * **Starred:** Browse songs you have marked as favorites. 
  
    **Note:** You can preview the song by tapping it.
5. (Optional) If you want to mark the song as a favorite and add it to the **Starred** section, tap the star icon next to the name of the song.
6. Tap the right arrow icon next to the song to select it.
   The Status editng screen appears. The song starts playing automatically. 
7. (Optional) Customize your Status using the following options:
    * **Change how the song appears on the Status:** Tap the music icon to see the available styles. Select one of the following options and then tap **Done**: 
      * **Aa:** Display the lyrics in sync with playback. 
      * **Vertical layout** (<img src="/Images/Vertical_music_sticker.png" width="35" height="25" />)**:** Display the cover art of the song in a vertical layout.
      * **Horizontal layout** (<img src="/Images/Horizontal_music_sticker.png" width="35" height="33" />)**:** Display the  cover art of the song in a horizontal layout. 
      * **Vinyl** (<img src="/Images/Vinyl_music_sticker.png" width="25" height="25" />)**:** Display the cover art of the song as an animated vinyl record.
      * **Cassette** (<img src="/Images/Cassette_music_sticker.png" width="25" height="25" />)**:** Display the cover art of the song as an animated cassette.   
   * **Stickers and emojis:** Tap the sticker icon to add stickers and emojis.   
   * **Text:** Tap **Aa** to add text. 
   You can change the font, size, color, layout, and background of this text.
   * **Draw:** Tap the pencil icon to draw. You can adjust the color and line size. 
   * **Caption**: Tap **Add a caption…** to add a line of description.
  8. (Optional) Tap **@** to mention up to five contacts in your Status.
  9. (Optional) On the same screen, select your Status audience by performing the following actions:   
   a. In the bottom-left corner of the screen, tap **Status**.  
   &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The **Choose audience** bottom sheet appears.  
   b. Select one of the following options:   
  
      * **My contacts:** Allow all your WhatsApp contacts to view your Status.
      * **My contacts except:** Exclude specific contacts from viewing your Status.
      * **Only share with…:** Select specific contacts who can view your Status. 
      
       **Note:** Tap the two-arrow icon next to **Share** to allow your audience to share your Status.     
      c. Tap **Share**.
10.  Tap the share icon at the bottom-right corner.

The selected song appears as your Status. Your audience can view and react to it.
