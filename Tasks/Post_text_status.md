# Post a text Status   

The Status feature allows you to share different types of media with your contacts. The following instructions describe how to post a text Status.  

To post a text Status,   

1. On WhatsApp, tap **Updates**.
2. On the **Updates** screen, perform one of the following actions:
   *   Tap **Add status**.
   *   Tap the camera icon at the bottom-right corner.
   A screen displays thumbnails of the media on your device, along with the available Status options.
3. Tap **Text**.   
4. On the next screen, type the desired message in the text field.   
**Note:** Your message can contain up to 700 characters.
1. (Optional) Customize your text Status using the following options found on the same screen:   
   * **Font style:** Tap **Aa** to change the font style of your message.
   * **Background color:** Tap the palette icon to change the background color of your Status.
2. (Optional) Tap **@** to mention up to five contacts in your Status.
3. (Optional) On the sames screenn, select your Status audience by performing the following actions:         
     
    a. In the bottom-left corner of the screen, tap **Status**.   
    
    &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The **Choose audience** bottom sheet appears.   

    b. Select one of the following options:      

     * **My contacts:** Allow all your WhatsApp contacts to view your Status.
     * **My contacts except:** Exclude specific contacts from viewing your Status.
     *  **Only share with…:** Select specific contacts who can view your Status.   
  
    **Note:** Tap the two-arrow icon next to **Share** to allow your audience to reshare your Status.

    c. Tap **Share**.

4. Tap the share icon at the bottom-right corner.   

Your text appears as your Status. Your audience can view and react to it.
