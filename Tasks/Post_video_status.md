# Post a video Status   

You can use the Status feature to share a video with your contacts. The procedure below explains how to post a video Status.   

**Prerequisite**   

A video on your device if you want to post an existing video.   

To post a video Status,   

1. On WhatsApp, tap **Updates**.
2. On the **Updates** screen, perform one of the following actions:    

   * Tap **Add status**.
   * Tap the camera icon at the bottom-right corner.
   A screen displays thumbnails of the media on your device, along with the available Status options.   

3. Select a video using one of the following methods:
  
   *   **From media:** Select a video from the displayed media.
   *   **From album:** Tap the **Recents** drop-down menu under the other Status options and select a video from your desired album.
   *   **From photo picker:** Tap the folder icon at the bottom-right corner and select a video from the displayed media.
   *   **From camera:** Tap **Camera** and record a video.
   *   **From camera screen:** Tap **Camera** and then tap the gallery icon at the bottom-left corner. Perform one of the following actions:
       *   Select a video from the displayed media.
       *    Tap **Recents** and select a video from the desired album.
       *   Tap the folder icon at the bottom and select a video from the displayed media.   
   
    **Note:** Your video can be up to 90 seconds long. 
    WhatsApp supports 3GP and MPEG-4 formats.
  
4. (Optional) After selecting the video, customize it using the following options:
      
   * **Caption**: Tap **Add a caption…** to add a line of description.
   * **Music:** Tap the music icon to add a song.
   * **Stickers and emojis:** Tap the sticker icon to add stickers and emojis.
   * **Text:** Tap **Aa** to add text on your video. 
   You can change the font, size, color, layout, and background of this text.
   * **Draw:** Tap the pencil icon to draw on your video. You can adjust the color and line size.
   * **Trim:** Drag the sliders on the video timeline bar to share a portion of the video.   
  **Note:** If you trim the video to six seconds or less, the **GIF** icon appears. Tap the icon to convert your video into a GIF. 
   * **Mute:** Tap the sound icon to disable the original sound of the video.
5. (Optional) Tap **@** to mention up to five contacts in your Status.
6. (Optional) On the same screen, select your Status audience by performing the following actions:
   
   a. In the bottom-left corner of the screen, tap **Status**.  
   &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The **Choose audience** bottom sheet appears.  
   b. Select one of the following options:
   
   * **My contacts:** Allow all your WhatsApp contacts to view your Status.
   * **My contacts except:** Exclude specific contacts from viewing your Status.
   * **Only share with…:** Select specific contacts who can view your Status.   
   
   **Note:** Tap the two-arrow icon next to **Share** to allow your audience to share your Status.     
    
  
   c. Tap **Share**.   
    
7. Tap the share icon at the bottom-right corner.

Your video appears as your Status. Your audience can view and react to it.
