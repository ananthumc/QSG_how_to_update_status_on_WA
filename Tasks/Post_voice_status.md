# Post a voice Status

WhatsApp allows you to share a voice recording as your Status. The following procedure explains how to post a voice recording as your Status.   

To post a voice Status,

1. On WhatsApp, tap **Updates**.
2. On the **Updates** screen, perform one of the following actions:            
   * Tap **Add status**.
   * Tap the camera icon at the bottom-right corner.
   A screen displays thumbnails of the media on your device, along with the available Status options.
3. Tap **Voice** from the list of Status options.
4. On the next screen, hold the microphone icon at the bottom-right to record your message. 
Release the icon to finish recording.
   **Note:** Slider your finger to the left while holding the microphone icon to cancel the recording. 
   You can record up to 1 minute and 30 seconds. 
5. (Optional) Tap the play icon to preview your recording.
If you want to discard the recording and record again, tap the delete icon.
6. (Optional) Tap the palette icon at the top-right of the screen to change the background color of your Status.
7. (Optional) Tap **@** to mention up to five contacts in your Status.
8. (Optional) On the same screen, select your Status audience by performing the following actions:    
    a. In the bottom-left corner of the screen, tap **Status**.   
    &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The **Choose audience** bottom sheet appears.   
    b. Select one of the following options:    
     * **My contacts:** Allow all your WhatsApp contacts to view your Status.
     * **My contacts except:** Exclude specific contacts from viewing your Status.
     *  **Only share with…:** Select specific contacts who can view your Status.      
    **Note:** Tap the two-arrow icon next to **Share** to allow your audience to share your Status.   

    c. Tap **Share**.     
9. Tap the share icon at the bottom-right corner.   

Your voice recording appears as your Status. Your audience can view and react to it.
